# Pipeline-in-a-Box — One-Page Pitch

*Use this as-is for a website section, a LinkedIn featured post, or to paste into a DM
when someone asks "wait, what do you actually do." Written for a business owner, not an
engineer — no jargon that requires a follow-up question.*

---

## The problem

Your numbers live in five different places — an invoicing tool, a scheduling app, a
spreadsheet somebody built three years ago, maybe QuickBooks. Nobody has time to pull
them together, so decisions get made on gut feel or a Friday-afternoon spreadsheet
scramble. By the time you know a problem exists, it's already cost you money.

## What Pipeline-in-a-Box is

A done-for-you system that pulls your data out of wherever it's stuck, puts it
somewhere it's actually safe and organized, and hands you back one dashboard that
updates itself — plus a short written note every week telling you what changed and why
it matters. You don't touch spreadsheets. You don't learn a new tool. You open one
link, or you read one email.

Think of it less like "buying software" and more like hiring someone to be your data
person for a few hours a month — without the cost of an actual hire.

## The two ways to start

**Starter — $750 setup, $300/month**
For a business running on 1-2 systems (say, your scheduling tool and your billing
tool). One clean dashboard. Good for getting your first real read on what's actually
happening in the business.

**Growth — $2,000 setup, $750/month**
For a business juggling more sources — multiple locations, multiple systems, or
numbers that need to be caught the moment something looks off. Everything from
Starter, plus more data sources pulled together, a refresh schedule built around how
you actually run the business, and alerts when something needs your attention before
it becomes a problem.

Both include: the pipeline build, the dashboard, and a weekly written insight note —
no extra software for you to buy, no IT project, no "figure it out yourself" manual.

## Why now

Every one of these systems already generates the data — it's just sitting scattered
and unused. The tools to pull it together affordably (cloud data warehouses, modern
dashboards) used to be enterprise-only and expensive. They aren't anymore. The gap
right now isn't the technology — it's that nobody's built the bridge for a business
your size. That's the bridge.

## Who it's for

Businesses that are cash-driven and outgrowing spreadsheets but aren't ready (or
willing) to hire a full-time analyst — auto shops, home care and elder care agencies,
accounting and tax firms, aviation service businesses (FBOs, maintenance shops, flight
schools), and similar operators who know their numbers matter but don't have time to
wrangle them.

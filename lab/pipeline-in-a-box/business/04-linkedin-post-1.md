## Notes for posting

- Post native (not a link out) — LinkedIn's algorithm favors posts that keep people on
  the platform.
- First comment can be a soft CTA if engagement is low after an hour: "If you want the
  play-by-play as this goes, follow along — I'll be posting weekly."
- Tag nothing/no one on post #1 — let it stand on its own; save tags for when someone
  specific becomes relevant (a client, a collaborator).
- Best posting window: early morning (6:30-8:30am) or lunch (11:30am-1pm) on a weekday
  for a US professional audience.

# First-Touch Outreach Templates

Rules for using these:
- Swap `[bracketed]` text per person/segment. Don't send a template untouched — change
  at least the specific detail (their business, tool, or a real observation) or it reads
  like a mail-merge.
- Keep it short. If it's longer than what's below, cut it before sending.
- Goal of message #1 is a reply or a 15-minute call, not a pitch — don't attach pricing
  or a deck up front.
- Send in small batches (4-5/day), not one blast — you want to actually have the
  follow-up conversations.

---

## Template A — Warm, direct network DM

*For: people you actually know (colleagues, DJ/wheelie audience, friends who run a
business or know someone who does).*

> Hey [name] — random question. I'm building a side thing where I take a small
> business's scattered data (spreadsheets, [their tool], whatever) and turn it into one
> dashboard that updates itself, plus a quick weekly note on what's going on. Doing it
> for [aviation shops / auto shops / home care agencies / accounting firms] to start.
> Does that sound useful for [their business], or do you know anyone it might? No
> pressure either way, just casting a wide net this week.

---

## Template B — Warm, "ask for the intro" version

*For: someone you know who isn't the buyer but might know one — a colleague at the
airline, a friend in the industry.*

> Hey [name] — I'm kicking off a small side business this month: done-for-you data
> pipelines + dashboards for small operators (starting with [aviation/auto/home care/
> accounting] since that's where I've got the most context). You know a lot of people in
> [their world] — is there anyone who comes to mind who's always complaining about not
> knowing their numbers, or drowning in spreadsheets at month-end? Happy to buy you a
> coffee for the intro if it goes anywhere.

---

## Template C — Cold LinkedIn DM

*For: prospects found via Sales Navigator, association directories, or Google — no
prior relationship. Keep it under 60 words.*

> Hi [name] — noticed [specific detail: "you run [business name]" / "[business] has a
> few locations" / saw a post they made about being busy]. I build simple dashboards for
> businesses like yours — pulls your data out of [the tools they likely use, e.g.
> "scheduling and billing"] into one view that updates itself, plus a short weekly note
> on what changed. Worth a quick 15-min call to see if it'd actually help, or is this not
> a pain point for you right now?

---

## Template D — Cold email / longer LinkedIn message

*For: when you have an email address (association directory, website contact) or want
to give slightly more context than a DM allows.*

> Subject: Quick question about [business name]'s reporting
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I came across [business name] through [ARSA's directory / the AOPA airport directory /
> ASA's shop finder / NSA's directory / a mutual connection] — [one specific,
> non-generic detail about them if you have one].
>
> I'm Derrick — data engineer by trade, currently building a small service for
> businesses like yours: I take whatever you're already tracking (billing, scheduling,
> maintenance logs, whatever it is) and turn it into one dashboard that updates itself
> automatically, plus a short written note each week telling you what actually changed
> and why it matters. No new software to learn, no IT project on your end.
>
> Not sure if this is a pain point for you right now, but if it is, I'd love 15 minutes
> to hear how you're tracking things today and see if it's worth building out.
>
> Either way, appreciate you reading this far.
>
> Derrick

## Segment swap-in cheatsheet (drop into `[the tools they likely use]` / `[specific detail]`)

| Segment | Likely tools/pain to reference | Directory-sourced credibility line |
|---|---|---|
| Aviation (FBO/MRO/flight school) | fuel sales, hangar rent, maintenance logs, instruction hours across separate systems | "saw [business] listed with ARSA" / "found you through AOPA's airport directory" |
| Auto repair | shop management tool (Tekmetric/Shopmonkey/Mitchell1) + separate accounting | "found [shop] through ASA's shop directory" |
| Home health/elder care | caregiver scheduling tool + billing, manual family/referral reports | "saw [agency] is a member of [state] Association for Home Care" |
| Accounting/tax | client reporting still built manually in Excel/exported PDFs | "found you through NSA's directory" — or pitch the referral/white-label angle: "thought this might be something you'd want to offer your own clients" |

## Follow-up rule

If no reply after 4-5 days, one short bump is fine — not a re-pitch, just a nudge:
> Hey [name] — just floating this back up in case it got buried. No worries if now's not
> the time.

Two touches total, then move on. Don't chase past that — time is better spent on the
next name on the list.

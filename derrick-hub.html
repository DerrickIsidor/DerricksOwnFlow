One Derrick.
Many worlds.

I woke up one day and realised I was already living the life I used to pray for. That moment didn't make me stop wanting more — it made me move differently.

I'm building three things at once: a content brand around riding and mindset, a DJ career built on energy and feeling, and a data career that goes way beyond a W2. Each one feeds the others.

This hub is my digital second home. A place for anyone who is also building their dream life to find me, connect with me, and maybe build something together.
